ANSWER (two sentences):
  Between 2000 and 2009 the series' level rose x3.5 but its absolute seasonal swing moved only x1.2, so the swing fell from 83% to 28% of the level (corr of level with swing-as-%-of-level -0.83).
  So the seasonality is NOT growing in proportion to the series, and 'the series is just growing' does not explain the swing either: in this recession the extra unemployed were added year-round (additive), not as a bigger winter surge.
  Caveat: the residual test is weak for BOTH models (|rho| 0.13 additive, 0.03 multiplicative, both under 0.15) and slightly favours the multiplicative model, so it
  does not overturn the per-year result; the multiplicative decomposition also forces a constant
  proportion that the per-year numbers contradict. One series in one recession; it need not agree
  with the Week 1 flights case, and the disagreement is reported as found.
