Q4 - my rule for more than three categories:
  Three colours is the ceiling for colour alone. Past three I keep the three-slot palette and
  add a second channel instead of another hue: marker shape (vizlib.MARKERS), direct labels
  at the line ends, or small multiples / a sorted table. If two categories can only be told
  apart by colour, the chart is not finished.
